INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------
Morgan fingerprint bits encode the presence of specific atomic
neighborhoods (substructures) within a molecule. A high feature
importance score means the presence or absence of that particular
chemical substructure is a strong predictor of toxicity for the
corresponding biological assay target.

Higher bit index importance does NOT mean a compound is toxic —
it means that substructure is highly discriminative between
toxic and non-toxic compounds in the training data.
